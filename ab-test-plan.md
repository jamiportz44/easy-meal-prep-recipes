A/B Test Plan – MKTG:4400 

Website: Easy Meal Prep for Busy People 
Website URL: https://jamiportz44.github.io/easy-meal-prep-recipes/ 
Page: Homepage 
Test Element: Main homepage headline 

1. What I Am Testing 

I will test whether changing the main headline on my homepage increases visitors' interest in exploring my meal prep website. 

Version A (Current): "Meals for Busy People: Easy Meal Prep Made Simple" 

Version B (New): "Easy Meal Prep Ideas for Your Busy Schedule" 

The only difference between the two versions will be the main homepage headline. All other text, images, links, and page elements will remain exactly the same. 

2. Hypothesis and Reasoning 

My hypothesis is that Version B will receive higher interest ratings than Version A because it more clearly communicates what the website offers. Although the current headline mentions easy meal prep, the new headline directly emphasizes meal prep ideas for people with busy schedules. I believe this wording will help visitors better understand the purpose of the website and make them more interested in exploring the recipes. 

3. Model, Persona, and Testing Prompt 

Model: GPT-6. I will use the same model and settings for every trial. 

Persona: A busy college student who has a limited budget, a full schedule, and wants easy meal ideas that do not require a lot of time or preparation. 

Exact Prompt: 

"You are a busy college student looking for easy and affordable meal prep ideas. You have a limited budget and a busy schedule, so you want recipes that are simple and convenient. Visit the following website and evaluate its homepage from your perspective as a potential visitor. After reviewing the page, rate how interested you would be in exploring the recipes on a scale of 1 to 7, with 1 meaning not interested at all and 7 meaning extremely interested. Provide your numerical rating and one sentence explaining your decision." 

I will provide either Version A or Version B of the homepage to the AI visitor. Each trial will take place in a fresh conversation with no previous conversation history. 

Assignment: I will randomly assign visitors to Version A or Version B before each trial and record which version they receive. 

4. Primary Outcome 

My primary outcome will be the visitor's interest rating on a 1–7 scale. I will record the numerical rating for every trial and calculate the average rating for Version A and Version B. 

5. Sample Size and Timeline 

I will complete 100 total trials, with 50 visitors assigned to Version A and 50 visitors assigned to Version B. 

I plan to launch the experiment by November 17, 2026, and finish collecting all 100 responses by November 24, 2026. 

I will not stop collecting responses early, even if one version appears to be performing better. 

6. How I Will Compare Results 

I will calculate the average interest rating for each version and compare the two averages to determine which headline performed better. I will also conduct a statistical test to determine whether the difference between the two versions is statistically significant or could have occurred by chance. 

My hypothesis will be supported if Version B receives a higher average interest rating than Version A, with statistical evidence that the difference is unlikely to be due to chance.
