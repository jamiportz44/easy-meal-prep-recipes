Easy meal ideas for every part of your day

Good food does not have to mean a long recipe or a sink full of dishes. These flexible ideas use everyday ingredients and simple cooking methods to make busy mornings, lunches, and dinners easier.

Quick breakfasts

5 minutes

Peanut Butter Banana Oats

Ingredients: Rolled oats, milk or a milk alternative, one banana, peanut butter, and cinnamon.

Directions: Combine oats and milk in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave according to the oat package instructions, then top with sliced banana, peanut butter, and cinnamon.

Prep tip: Portion dry oats and cinnamon into jars ahead of time.

10 minutes

Veggie Egg Muffins

Ingredients: 6 eggs, ½ cup diced bell pepper, ¼ cup shredded cheese, salt, and pepper.

Directions: Beat the eggs, stir in the vegetables and cheese, and divide into greased muffin cups. Bake at 350°F until the centers are set, about 18–22 minutes.

Prep tip: Refrigerate cooked muffins for up to four days and reheat thoroughly.

Simple lunches

15 minutes

Chicken Pasta Salad

Ingredients: Cooked pasta, cooked chicken, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, olive oil, lemon juice, and dried Italian herbs.

Directions: Let the cooked pasta cool, then combine it with chopped vegetables and chicken. Toss with olive oil, lemon juice, and herbs.

Prep tip: Divide into individual containers and refrigerate promptly.

10 minutes

Crunchy Chickpea Wrap

Ingredients: 1 can of chickpeas, whole-wheat tortillas, shredded lettuce, grated carrots, plain yogurt, and lemon juice.

Directions: Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Lightly mash them with yogurt and lemon juice, then add lettuce and carrots to each tortilla and roll it up.

Prep tip: Keep the filling separate from the tortillas until you are ready to eat.

Easy dinners

25 minutes

One-Pot Tomato Pasta

Ingredients: 8 ounces pasta, 1 can diced tomatoes, 2 cups broth, 2 cloves garlic, and 2 handfuls of spinach.

Directions: Add pasta, tomatoes, broth, and minced garlic to a pot. Bring to a boil and simmer, stirring regularly, until the pasta is cooked and the liquid has mostly reduced. Add spinach and stir until wilted. Add a little water if needed.

Prep tip: Measure the dry ingredients before your week begins.

20 minutes

Sheet-Pan Sausage and Vegetables

Ingredients: 12 ounces fully cooked sausage, 1 bell pepper, 1 zucchini, 1 small red onion, olive oil, and Italian seasoning.

Directions: Heat the oven to 400°F. Slice the sausage and vegetables, toss with oil and seasoning, and spread on a sheet pan. Roast for about 20–25 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the sausage is heated through.

Prep tip: Chop the vegetables in advance and keep them refrigerated in a covered container.

Build a meal from what you have

Start with a grain such as rice or pasta, add a protein such as beans, eggs, or chicken, and finish with a vegetable and a flavorful sauce. This simple formula helps you use ingredients you already own instead of buying something new for every recipe.
