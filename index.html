Meet the people behind the guide

Easy Meal Prep Guide was created by fictional home cooks Emma Brooks and Noah Miller. Emma loves finding creative uses for pantry staples, while Noah enjoys turning favorite comfort foods into practical weekday meals. Together, they share approachable ideas designed to make cooking feel less overwhelming.

Our philosophy is simple: you do not need fancy equipment or an elaborate menu to eat well at home. A little planning and a few reliable recipes can go a long way.
