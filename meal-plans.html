Your week, organized

A five-day meal plan made simple

Give yourself a head start with five easy dinners that reuse ingredients, keep your grocery list manageable, and help you make the most of your time in the kitchen.

Planning tip: This sample plan serves approximately two people per dinner. Adjust portions and ingredients to fit your household.

Your weekday dinner schedule

Monday

Sheet-Pan Chicken and Vegetables

Roast chicken breast, broccoli, and chopped potatoes with olive oil, garlic, paprika, salt, and pepper. Serve everything from one pan.

Prep ahead: Chop the vegetables and mix the seasonings.

Tuesday

Black Bean Taco Bowls

Combine cooked rice, black beans, corn, diced tomatoes, shredded lettuce, and a spoonful of salsa. Add cheese or avocado if you like.

Prep ahead: Cook rice and portion the toppings.

Wednesday

Chicken and Vegetable Wraps

Fill whole-wheat tortillas with cooked chicken, lettuce, shredded carrots, and a simple yogurt dressing.

Prep ahead: Use leftover cooked chicken stored safely from Monday.

Thursday

Tomato and Spinach Pasta

Toss cooked pasta with canned diced tomatoes, spinach, garlic, olive oil, and a sprinkle of Parmesan cheese.

Prep ahead: Measure the dry pasta and seasonings together.

Friday

Easy Fried Rice

Stir-fry cooked rice with mixed vegetables, scrambled egg, and low-sodium soy sauce. Add leftover vegetables that need to be used.

Prep ahead: Portion the vegetables and sauce. Cool and refrigerate cooked rice promptly.

Weekly grocery list

Check your pantry and refrigerator before shopping. You may already have several of these ingredients.

Produce

  • 1 head of broccoli
  • 3 medium potatoes
  • 1 bag of spinach
  • 1 head of lettuce
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion and 1 bulb of garlic
  • 1 avocado, optional

Protein and Dairy

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 can of black beans
  • 4 eggs
  • Plain yogurt
  • Shredded cheese or Parmesan

Grains and Pantry Items

  • Rice
  • Whole-wheat tortillas
  • Pasta
  • 1 can of corn
  • 1 can of diced tomatoes
  • Salsa and soy sauce
  • Olive oil, paprika, and black pepper

Make the plan work for you

Swap meals between days when your schedule changes. Freeze portions you will not eat within a few days, and refrigerate cooked food promptly in shallow containers. Keep raw chicken separate from ready-to-eat foods and cook it to 165°F (74°C).
